FAQs

Everything you need to know about AdoTeam and how it simplifies HR for growing businesses.

What is AdoTeam?

AdoTeam is an integrated HRMS developed by AdoMantra. It brings employee records, attendance, leave, payroll, documents, tasks, and events into one platform, so HR teams can manage everyday work more easily.

Who is AdoTeam designed for?

AdoTeam is built for small businesses, startups, growing companies, and SMEs that want structured HR processes without unnecessary complexity.

Can employees access their own information?

Yes. Through the employee self-service portal, employees can view and update relevant profile details, check leave balances, access payslips, view tasks, and receive notifications.

How does leave management work in AdoTeam?

Employees apply for leave directly on the platform. Managers review and approve requests, and leave balances can be tracked in real time, so there's no need for email follow-ups.

Does AdoTeam support attendance tracking?

Yes. HR teams get real-time visibility into attendance and punctuality. Attendance information can also connect with leave and payroll processes, reducing manual cross-checking.

Can employees download their payslips?

Yes. Employees can view and download payslips directly from their dashboard, without requesting them from HR.

How can I get started with AdoTeam?

Get in touch with the AdoMantra team to learn more or request a demo. They'll walk you through the platform and help you find the right fit for your business.
